WE WEAR GREEN.

Green is not a rank. It is a promise: we are here to learn, not to loot.

Green Hat Crew started as a group chat of people who kept asking "wait, how does that actually work?" and never stopped. We are students, tinkerers and night-shift debuggers who treat security like a playground, not a weapon.

A green hat means beginner energy with zero shame. We ask the basic question. We break our own things first. We write down what happened so the next person does not have to start from zero.

If you are looking for a crew that flexes zero-days on strangers, keep walking. If you want to build a home lab, brick a VM, restore a snapshot, and laugh about it at 2am, pull up a chair.

  • Own it or skip it. Your hardware, your lab, your CTF box. Someone else's system is never a target.
  • Learn loudly. Ask the basic question. Confusion is just the tutorial loading screen.
  • Document everything. Screenshots are not a write-up. Notes turn chaos into skill.
  • Share the knowledge. Gatekeeping is dead. Teach the next beginner what you just learned.

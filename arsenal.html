RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

LAB RULES, NO EXCEPTIONS.

// OWN IT OR SKIP IT

Your hardware, your lab, your CTF box. Someone else's system is never a target.

// WRITE IT DOWN

If it is not documented, it did not happen. Notes turn chaos into actual skill.

// SHARE THE KNOWLEDGE

Gatekeeping is dead. Every trick we learn gets explained to the next beginner.
